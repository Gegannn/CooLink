Comprehensive Technical Overview

CooLink - Daily-Wage Worker Marketplace

Connecting skilled workers with contractors & households in Kerala without the morning bus stand struggle.

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

In India's informal labour market, millions of daily-wage workers must wake before dawn and physically travel to bus stands, railway stations, or road junctions (e.g. "thozhilali junctions" in Kerala), waiting in uncertainty hoping a contractor selects them for work.

The Traditional Morning Stand

  • Workers spend bus fare with zero guarantee of employment.
  • Wages, location, and job requirements are unknown until arriving at the work site.
  • High vulnerability to wage theft and exploitation.
  • No digital record of skills, experience, or ratings.

The CooLink Solution

  • Workers tap "AVAILABLE TODAY" from their phone at home.
  • Contractors discover verified workers by trade, rating & locality.
  • Wages & scope agreed upfront before accepting.
  • Verified government identity badge & tamper-proof reviews.

2. Key Innovations & Features Built

  • 1-Tap Live Availability Engine: Single-touch toggles for AVAILABLE, BUSY, and NOT AVAILABLE so workers signal work readiness instantly.
  • Atomic Concurrency Lock: Prevents double-booking. When a worker accepts job request A, their status atomically locks to BUSY and competing requests for that date are cancelled.
  • Location Privacy: Worker residential GPS coordinates are masked by adding a ~2 km geographical fuzzing offset ("Within 5 km of Kottakkal") to protect personal security.
  • Bi-directional Trust & Reviews: Only workers and contractors who participated in a completed job can review each other; duplicate reviews are blocked.
  • Spam-Free Communication: Contact details and in-app chat are locked until a work request is accepted.
  • Kerala Bilingual Support: 1-click toggle between English and Malayalam (മേസ്തിരി പണി, ടൈൽ വർക്ക്, ഇലക്ട്രിക്കൽ, പ്ലംബിംഗ്, ആശാരി പണി, etc.).
